## The story

- Why they came to us: Build a detective-themed mobile game combining storytelling with engaging gameplay while supporting monetization.   (source: deck)
- What was hard: Managing game progression, integrating third-party services, optimizing advertisement performance, and maintaining immersive gameplay.   (source: deck)
- How we solved it: Developed a structured game architecture supporting investigation mechanics, advertisements, and scalable future content.   (source: deck)
- Technical highlights: Modular architecture; Scalable level management; Optimized performance   (source: deck)
- Architecture: Flutter → Game Engine → Google Ads → Third-party Services   (source: deck)
- Business value: Establishes a flexible foundation for expanding game content while maintaining enjoyable gameplay.   (source: deck)
- What we decided and why: Story-driven games need a content pipeline that outlives the initial release. Structuring missions as reusable modules makes future updates significantly easier, reduces maintenance cost, and supports continuous content growth.   (source: owner)
- What was delivered: Detective-themed 2D mobile game with ad monetisation and third-party service integrations, tuned to keep gameplay smooth.   (source: projects.ts)
